# UX & Product Design Learning Resources

A curated collection of resources, books, cheat sheets, and guidelines for learning user experience design, frontend polish, and interaction principles.

---

## 1. Usability Heuristics & Foundations

* **[Nielsen Norman Group — 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/)**  
  The ultimate authority on digital usability. Read about visibility of system status, user control, error prevention, and recognition vs. recall.
* **[Nielsen Norman Group — Emergency Exits: User Control and Freedom](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/emergency-exits/)**  
  Why users need clear ways out of every flow, modal, or process.
* **[Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) by Jon Yablonski**  
  A beautifully designed interactive encyclopedia of psychological UX laws (Fitts's Law, Hick's Law, Jakob's Law, Miller's Law, Doherty Threshold).

---

## 2. Practical UI Design for Developers

* **[Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com/) by Adam Wathan & Steve Schoger**  
  Written by the creators of Tailwind CSS. Specifically tailored for developers who have no design degree. Teaches visual hierarchy, spacing systems, color palettes, and component design tactics.
* **[The Nine States of Design](https://medium.com/swlh/the-nine-states-of-design-5b7bce09e752) by Vince Speelman**  
  The definitive article on why every UI component must handle empty, loading, partial, error, and active states.
* **[Don't Make Me Think (Revisited)](https://www.sensible.com/dmmt.html) by Steve Krug**  
  The classic book on common-sense web usability and intuitive navigation.

---

## 3. Micro-Interactions & Motion Design

* **[Framer Motion Documentation](https://motion.dev/)**  
  Official docs and examples for layout animations, exit animations, gestures, and springs in React.
* **[Microinteractions: Designing with Details](https://www.daniffert.com/books/microinteractions/) by Dan Saffer**  
  How small interaction details (button click feedback, toggle switches, subtle chimes) turn a clunky product into a polished, delightful app.
* **[UI Movement](https://uimovement.com/)**  
  Daily curated feed of top UI animation and micro-interaction examples.

---

## 4. Accessibility (a11y) & Contrast

* **[W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Quick Reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/)**  
  Reference for keyboard accessibility, color contrast ratios (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for UI elements), and ARIA semantics.
* **[WebAIM Contrast Checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/)**  
  Tool to verify color contrast between foreground text and background surfaces.
* **[Radix UI Primitives](https://www.radix-ui.com/primitives)**  
  Unstyled, accessible React components with built-in WAI-ARIA compliance, focus trapping, and keyboard controls.
